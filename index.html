Meet the Tarapirohe

Discover New Zealand’s agile, endemic black-fronted tern! Explore how our class combined ecological research with creative coding to build interactive games protecting this taonga species.

What We Learned Along the River

Before coding a single line, we researched what makes the Tarapirohe unique—and why braided river habitats need our protection.

🏞️

Braided River Habitats

Tarapirohe nest on dynamic shingle islands in braided rivers—shifting habitats shaped by water flow from the mountains to the sea.

🐟

River-to-Sea Food Webs

From aquatic insects and skinks inland to small fish along the coast, every link in the food web keeps the Tarapirohe thriving.

🛡️

Threats & Protection

Introduced predators, hydro dams, climate-driven floods, and human disturbance threaten nests—making conservation awareness vital.

💻

Game Design & Coding

We turned science into playable stories—designing mechanics, artwork, and challenges hosted live on GitHub Pages.
